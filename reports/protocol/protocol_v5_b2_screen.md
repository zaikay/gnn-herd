# B2 scaling screen — 1 November 2026

Preparation only; no GPU runs by the agent. Proposed tag: `protocol-v5-b2-screen`. Gate C acceptance for B remains unchanged. No test-block access.

**Scaling fixed before the screen:** for observed area i, `s_i = 1 + mean(y_i,t)` over training target months January 2011 through the fit origin, inclusive. Unknown areas get scale 1 and retain all masks. Compute once at each fit, save it, and hold it fixed for all training windows and later forecasts from that fit. Only fit-history data enter the scale; no anchor target month enters it. Earlier training samples use the fit's training-set preprocessing statistics, not a separately estimated sample-origin scale.

Counts and cumulative installed base are divided by s **before log1p**, preserving the existing six-channel design. Calendar/observation/isolate channels are unchanged. The B encoder/late neighbour correction and graph handling are unchanged. Multiply the softplus rate by s and apply the shared 1e-6 floor in raw-count units. The Poisson objective remains on original count targets; no scaled-target loss or area-weighted objective. Scales add buffers, not learned parameters. All-one scales reproduce original B features/outputs exactly. This is a scale-normalisation adaptation inspired by [DeepAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.04110), not adoption of its likelihood or sampling scheme.

**Primary screen:** two original tuning anchors (August 2019, August 2020), seed 11.
